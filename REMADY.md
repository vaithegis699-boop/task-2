## Key Insights
1. The average final grade of students is 10.42.
2. Students have an average of 5.71 absences.
3. Male students have a slightly higher average final grade (10.91) than female students (9.97).
4. Students with no previous failures have the highest average final grade (11.25). As failures increase, the average grade decreases.
5. Students in study time category 3 have the highest average final grade (11.40).

## Trends, Patterns and Anomalies
- Higher previous failures are associated with lower final grades.
- Study time shows differences in average final grades.
- Some students have unusually high absence values, which may be potential anomalies.

Conclusion
The EDA helped identify important patterns and relationships in student performance data. The analysis provides useful insights into grades, study time, failures and absences.
